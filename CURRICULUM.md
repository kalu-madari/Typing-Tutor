# KrutiDev 010 Typing Curriculum

This document outlines the complete learning curriculum for the KrutiDev typing application, designed to guide learners from absolute beginners to expert typists. The progression is slow, methodical, and builds muscle memory securely, inspired by TypingMaster and TypingClub.

## Overall Learning Roadmap
The curriculum is divided into stages, encompassing approximately **250+ individual lessons**, requiring an estimated **30-40 hours** of practice to complete.

## Difficulty Curve
1.  **Lessons 1-30 (Row Mastery):** Extremely low difficulty. High repetition. Focus is 100% on accuracy and muscle memory. Target WPM: 10-15.
2.  **Lessons 31-60 (Shift Key):** Moderate spike in difficulty. Slower pace. Target WPM drops slightly as cognitive load increases. Target WPM: 15-20.
3.  **Lessons 61-90 (Numbers & Symbols):** Moderate difficulty. Target WPM: 25-30.
4.  **Lessons 91-150 (Fluency):** Difficulty scales by length of words/sentences rather than new keys. Focus shifts to reading ahead. Target WPM: 30-40.
5.  **Lessons 150+ (Mastery):** High difficulty. Complex texts. Target WPM: 40-60+.
